The pet avatar maker turns one photo of your pet into a stylized cartoon avatar, sized square for profile pictures on Instagram, WhatsApp, Discord and everywhere else. It runs on the same engine as the [AI portrait studio](/#create) — this guide covers the style picker, photo choice, download rules, and what to do when a result isn't right yet.

## What you need

- One clear photo of your pet: JPG, PNG or WebP, under 5 MB, face visible and well lit.
- About thirty seconds per avatar.
- A free account is only needed to download the result — generating works without signing in. You get 3 free pictures per day, shared with the portrait studio.

## Creating your avatar, step by step

1. Open the [avatar maker](/tools/pet-avatar) and **pick a style**. Three are available (comparison below) — every style keeps your pet's real fur color and markings.
2. **Add a photo** by tapping the dashed area and choosing from your device. One photo is enough; the tool shows a small preview so you can confirm the right file.
3. Press **Make my avatar**. Generation takes about thirty seconds; the progress panel keeps you company.
4. Review the result. Happy? Move to the next step. Not quite? Try another style — each generation uses one of your daily free pictures.
5. **Download** with the button under the avatar. Downloads are protected by a free sign-in (Google) — the same rule as the portrait studio, and it's what keeps your pictures recoverable later.
6. Loop back to step 1 to try a different style, or head to the studio for a [full-size portrait](/#create) of the same pet.

## Choosing between the three styles

- **Cartoon 3D** — big glossy eyes, rounded shapes, soft studio light. The safest pick for profile pictures: readable at small sizes and universally likable.
- **Kawaii chibi** — oversized head, tiny body, bold outlines. Maximum cuteness, perfect for sticker-like avatars and younger audiences.
- **Watercolor** — loose painterly washes on cream paper. The most "art gallery" of the three; lovely for memorial avatars and softer feeds.

## Getting a result that looks like them

The style does the drawing; the photo does the likeness. A face-filling, well-lit, sharp photo produces the strongest avatar — the same rules as full portraits, and our [photo mistakes guide](/blog/why-your-ai-pet-portrait-doesnt-look-like-them) covers all seven errors in detail. The short version: face big in frame, light on the face, no motion blur, pet alone.

## Troubleshooting

**"You've used all 3 free pictures."** The counter resets every day. If you're mid-session and out of pictures, [Pro](/pricing) removes the daily cap and the watermark on downloads.

**The avatar doesn't look like my pet.** Nine times out of ten the photo is the cause — blurry, too dark, or face too small. Re-shoot following the rules above, then regenerate. Trying a different style also changes the likeness feel dramatically.

**Generation failed.** Rare, but the studio can hiccup under load. Failures don't consume your daily quota — just press the button again.

**Download asks me to sign in.** That's expected: generating is open to everyone, downloading requires a free Google sign-in so your pictures stay attached to you. We only receive your name, email and profile picture.

**Is my photo used to train AI?** No. Photos are used only to paint your avatar, never to train models, and uploads are deleted automatically within seven days. The full story is on our [AI disclosure](/ai-disclosure) page.

## What to do with the avatar

It's a square 1:1 image, ready for any profile picture field. It also makes a delightful group-chat sticker — and if your pet deserves the full renaissance treatment after this, the [template center](/templates) is one tap away.
