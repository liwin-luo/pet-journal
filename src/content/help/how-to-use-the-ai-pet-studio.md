The studio is the heart of PetsDaily: you describe a picture, attach photos of your pet, and an AI paints it — a renaissance portrait, a Christmas card, a movie poster. This manual covers every part of the flow: the message box, templates, photos, the daily free quota, downloads, and what to do when something doesn't go as planned.

## What you need

- 1 to 4 photos of your pet (JPG, PNG or WebP, each under 5 MB). One clear face-on shot is enough; more angles improve fur and eye detail.
- A sentence describing what you want — or a template that already contains the perfect sentence.
- About thirty seconds. No account needed to generate; a free Google sign-in only when you download.

## The studio flow, step by step

1. Open the [studio](/#create) on the homepage.
2. **Describe your wish** in the message box: a scene, an outfit, an art style, a mood. "My cat as a renaissance general, oil painting, dramatic light" works. Keep it about your pet — requests involving people or unsafe content are filtered out automatically.
3. Or skip writing entirely: pick a **template** from the row under the box. Each template carries a proven, professional-grade prompt — tap one and just add your twist.
4. **Attach photos** with the paperclip. One is fine; up to four help the model nail fur patterns and eye color from different angles.
5. Press **Generate**. The studio rewrites your words into a detailed artistic brief, then paints the picture — about thirty seconds, with progress messages to keep you company.
6. Review the result. Not perfect? **Regenerate** for free — it's the fastest fix and uses one of your daily pictures only when a generation succeeds.
7. **Download** your picture (free Google sign-in keeps it attached to you), or **post it to the gallery** to share it with other pet people — gallery posts are reviewed before going public.
8. Can't find the picture you just made? The **"Your pictures"** strip at the top of the studio keeps your recent results — before and after signing in.

## Writing wishes that paint better

Concrete beats abstract, always. Name the art style ("oil painting", "watercolor"), the scene ("by a fireplace", "in a garden"), and one mood word. Then stop — the studio expands your sentence into the full professional prompt itself. For the complete philosophy, read the [AI pet portrait guide](/blog/ai-pet-portraits-complete-guide); for the photo side, the [seven photo mistakes](/blog/why-your-ai-pet-portrait-doesnt-look-like-them) explain most disappointing results.

## The free quota, plainly

You get **3 pictures a day**, every day. Only successful generations count — failures and blocked requests are free. The counter resets daily, and it's shared between the studio and the [avatar maker](/tools/pet-avatar). If you want unlimited generations and watermark-free downloads, that's what [Pro](/pricing) is for.

## Troubleshooting

**My request was blocked.** The filter refuses requests involving people (especially children), violence, explicit content, celebrities and trademarks. Rephrase the wish to be only about your pet — the portraits are a pets-only studio by design.

**Generation failed.** The studio hiccupped under load. Failed attempts don't consume quota — press Generate again, usually with a clearer photo.

**My photo was rejected.** Check format (JPG/PNG/WebP) and size (under 5 MB per photo). iPhone HEIC files need converting first — emailing the photo to yourself as a JPG takes ten seconds.

**The result doesn't look like my pet.** This is almost always a photo issue, and it's fixable: see the [photo mistakes guide](/blog/why-your-ai-pet-portrait-doesnt-look-like-them), then regenerate.

**Download is locked.** Sign in with Google — it takes one click, keeps your pictures recoverable, and we never see your password.

**Where do my photos go?** They're used only to paint your picture, never to train AI, and they're deleted automatically within seven days. The complete policy lives on the [AI disclosure](/ai-disclosure) page.

When your first portrait turns out — and it will — the [gallery](/gallery) is full of pets who got there before yours.
