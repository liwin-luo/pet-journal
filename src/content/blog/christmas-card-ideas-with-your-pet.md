A Christmas card with your actual pet on it beats a stock card every single time — and with AI you can shoot it tonight from photos you already have. Below are 25 ideas, grouped by mood, that you can make free with [PetsDaily's holiday templates](/templates?cat=holiday). One clear photo of your pet's face is all each one needs.

## Classic & cozy

1. **The fireplace portrait** — your pet by a glowing fire with stockings hung behind. The all-time bestseller. Start from the [Christmas template](/templates/xmas).
2. **Santa's little helper** — a Santa hat, a calm expression, snowy bokeh.
3. **Holiday sweater session** — an ugly-but-loved knit sweater, warm indoor light.
4. **Cocoa by the window** — snow falling outside, paws on the windowsill.
5. **The family photo stand-in** — your pet posed like the official family portrait, everyone else cropped out.

## Elegant & artsy

6. **Renaissance royalty in a wreath** — oil-painting style with a holly crown. Combines two crowd favorites: [royal portrait](/templates/royal) + Christmas.
7. **The winter noble** — velvet cloak, dark forest backdrop, painterly light.
8. **Golden hour snow portrait** — backlight, drifting snowflakes, cinematic mood.
9. **Vintage postcard** — your pet on a 1900s-style Christmas postcard, faded edges.
10. **Stained glass window** — your pet rendered as a cathedral window panel.

## Funny & meme-worthy

11. **Santa hat, zero enthusiasm** — the hat is on; the soul has left. Works for every cat.
12. **Caught eating the Christmas dinner** — guilty eyes, empty plate.
13. **Gift-wrapped disaster** — your pet tangled in tinsel and string lights (drawn safely, of course).
14. **The elf who quit** — tiny elf costume, thousand-yard stare.
15. **Sleigh security officer** — sunglasses, clipboard, guarding the presents.
16. **Reindeer interview** — your pet as the reindeer who didn't make the team.

## Cards built for printing

17. **The front-page news** — a vintage newspaper: "LOCAL HERO SAVES CHRISTMAS".
18. **Stamp collection** — your pet as a series of holiday postage stamps.
19. **Storybook illustration** — a children's-book page: "Twas the night before Christmas…"
20. **The advent calendar reveal** — 24 tiny doors, your pet behind door 25.
21. **Concert poster** — "WOOFSTOCK — Live on Christmas Eve", your pet as the headliner.

## For the whole family

22. **Stocking guardians** — one portrait per pet, each above their own stocking.
23. **First Christmas together** — a soft, painterly portrait to mark a rescue's first holiday.
24. **Far-away gift card** — for relatives overseas: your pet "signing" the card with a paw print.
25. **The gotcha-day anniversary card** — Christmas adoption anniversaries deserve their own card.

## How to make one in about a minute

1. Pick one clear, well-lit photo of your pet's face — phone photos are fine.
2. Open the [generator](/#create), choose a template like [Xmas](/templates/xmas), and attach the photo.
3. Add one twist if you like ("with a tiny scarf"), generate, and download. The [AI pet portrait guide](/blog/ai-pet-portraits-complete-guide) explains the whole process if it's your first time.

Print the result at your local photo shop as a 5×7 card, or post it straight to the family group chat — either way, it's the only card this year with your actual animal on it.

Want the funny version instead? Turn any photo into a share-ready [pet meme in your browser](/tools/meme-maker) — free, no sign-up, nothing uploaded.
